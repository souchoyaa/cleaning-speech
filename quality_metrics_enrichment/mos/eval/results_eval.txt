HEADLINE — no single metric covers every failure mode (the benchmark's purpose):
  * naturalness (Griffin-Lim, clean SNR) : UTMOS 1.00 across severities, but
    DNSMOS blind (0.76; -> 0.58 at gl32).            => keep UTMOS
  * telephony (8 kHz band-limit)         : only AudioBox PQ/CE catch (0.84/0.83);
    DNSMOS 0.60, SI-SDR 0.62 blind.                  => keep AudioBox
  * clipping                             : DNSMOS 0.87 best; PESQ misses (0.65).
  * crosstalk                            : STOI/UTMOS 0.98/0.99; DNSMOS weaker.
  * awgn / real_noise                    : DNSMOS/PESQ/PQ robust; SI-SDR & STOI
    collapse at mild noise (SI-SDR 0.52-0.65 at >=25 dB).
  * music                                : PC = 0.00 (perfect INVERTED detector) —
    PC is a content descriptor, never a quality penalty.
  * clean_control                        : all metrics ~0.50 — FLAC round-trip is
    transparent (sanity check passes).
